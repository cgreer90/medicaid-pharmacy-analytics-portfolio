\b\fs26 Portfolio Overview\b0\fs22\par
This portfolio demonstrates end-to-end knowledge of \b Medicaid Pharmacy Benefit Management (PBM)\b0  operations, claims adjudication, and health IT systems lifecycle. It bridges two critical dimensions of healthcare data:\par
\par
1. \b Retrospective Analysis (Audit & Analytics):\b0  Analyzing historical claims data to identify utilization spikes, outlier patterns, and policy non-compliance.\par
2. \b Prospective System Implementation (Business Analysis & UAT):\b0  Translating Medicaid policy changes into structured Business Requirements Documents (BRDs), NCPDP standard field mappings, UAT test matrices, and defect management workflows.\par

\b\fs26 Featured Projects\b0\fs22\par
\par
\b 1. {\field{\*\fldinst{HYPERLINK "https://github.com/cgreer90/medicaid-pharmacy-analytics-portfolio/tree/main/medicaid-pharmacy-analytics-portfolio/project-1-audit-risk-model"}}{\fldrslt{\cf1\ul Project 1: Michigan Medicaid Audit Risk Model}}}\b0\par
* \b Domain:\b0  Claims Auditing, Provider Profiling & Risk Scoring\par
* \b Focus:\b0  Retrospective analysis of outpatient Medicaid pharmacy claims to identify outliers, potential fraud/waste/abuse (FWA), and clinical policy compliance gaps.\par
* \b Key Deliverables:\b0\par
  - Risk-scoring methodology across high-cost classes (e.g., GLP-1s, specialty medications).\par
  - Data queries and automated rules to detect anomalies in prescription frequency and days supply.\par
  - Summary reporting on potential state budget exposure and policy compliance gaps.\par
\par
\b 2. {\field{\*\fldinst{HYPERLINK "https://github.com/cgreer90/medicaid-pharmacy-analytics-portfolio/tree/main/medicaid-pharmacy-analytics-portfolio/project-2-pa-rule-requirements"}}{\fldrslt{\cf1\ul Project 2: Prior Authorization Rule Change Package}}}\b0\par
* \b Domain:\b0  Pharmacy IT Business Analysis, POS Adjudication & UAT\par
* \b Focus:\b0  Point-of-Sale (POS) Prior Authorization, Clinical DUR edits, and UAT validation simulating a Medicaid policy update for GLP-1 medications.\par
* \b Key Deliverables:\b0\par
  - \b Business Requirements Document (BRD):\b0  Standardized specs defining triggers, ICD-10 diagnosis verification (BR-002), 180-day step-therapy lookbacks (BR-003), and quantity limits (BR-004).\par
  - \b NCPDP D.0 Standard Mapping:\b0  Field alignment for 407-D7 (NDC), 427-DR (Diagnosis Code), 442-E7 (Quantity Dispensed), and 405-D5 (Days Supply).\par
  - \b UAT Scenario Matrix:\b0  8-case testing package covering happy paths, negative rejections (Code 70, 76), step-therapy pends (Code 75), and edge cases.\par
  - \b Python Adjudication Prototype:\b0  Prototype script evaluating test claims against logic rules to generate automated pass/fail test reports.\par
  - \b Defect Log:\b0  JIRA/ADO-style defect entries documenting root cause, severity, and resolution verification.\par
